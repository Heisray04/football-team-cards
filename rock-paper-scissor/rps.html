Let's play Rock, Paper, and Scissor!

Here are the rules

The player has to click a button to select his choice.

A point is given for each win.

  • Rock smashes Scissor
  • Scissor cuts Paper
  • Paper covers Rock

If the player's selection and the computer's selections are the same (e.g., rock and rock), it's a tie and hence no point is given.

First to reach 3 rounds wins.

Goodluck!
